Richmond FAQ

Mobile detailing in Richmond, VA – common questions

Do you cover all of Richmond, or just certain neighborhoods?

All of it. Our mobile unit carries its own water and power, so we're not limited to areas with easy hose/outlet access – from the Fan and Church Hill to Manchester and the West End, if your car is parked in Richmond city limits, we can reach it.

How much does mobile car detailing cost in Richmond, VA?

Standard Detail starts at $160, Interior Only Deep Clean at $200, a Full Detail at $320, and Ceramic Coating at $500 – all sedan/coupe base rates. SUVs, trucks, and minivans run roughly 20–40% higher. Your exact price is confirmed before we start.

Can you detail my car at an apartment or office building downtown?

In most cases, yes. Let us know your parking situation when you book (surface lot, parking garage, street parking) and we'll confirm we can set up there before your appointment.

How do I book a mobile detail in Richmond?

Use the live calendar on our booking page to grab an open slot, or call/text us at (804) 555-0142 and we'll get you scheduled directly.
