Our Story

Detailing that fits your schedule, not a shop's

We started River City Mobile Detailing because the traditional model — drop your car off, wait around, hope the price you were quoted is the price you pay — didn't make sense for busy people in the Richmond area. So we built a fully mobile operation instead: we bring the water, the power, and the equipment straight to your driveway or office parking lot.

Every job is quoted upfront based on your vehicle's size and condition, confirmed before we start, and walked through with you when we're done. No shop, no hidden fees, no surprises.
