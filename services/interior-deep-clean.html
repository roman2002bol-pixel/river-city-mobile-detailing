Frequently Asked Questions

Can this remove pet hair and odors?

Yes — steam cleaning and shampooing are specifically effective on embedded pet hair and lingering odors. Let us know the specifics when booking.

Does this include the exterior?

No, this package is interior-only. If you want both, our Full Detail package covers interior and exterior together at a combined price.

How long does it take?

Typically 90 minutes to 2.5 hours depending on vehicle size and interior condition.
